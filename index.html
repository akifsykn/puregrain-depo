Workshop record

From hide to home.

Nothing here is bought finished and resold. Each piece passes through the same four stations of our workshop, and the same pair of hands.

Pure Grain · Istanbul Lot 01 — Selection

Every pelt chosen by hand

We buy whole natural hides, never offcuts. Each pelt is inspected for wool length, softness and grain before it enters the workshop.

Pure Grain · Istanbul Lot 02 — Tanning

Finished to stay soft

Hides are tanned and finished so the leather back stays supple and the wool keeps its natural sheen. For years, not seasons.

Pure Grain · Istanbul Lot 03 — Handcraft

Cut, backed and stitched at our bench

Pillows, throws, runners and rugs are cut and sewn in-house. Seams are stitched to be sat on, leaned on and lived with.

Pure Grain · Istanbul Lot 04 — Delivery

Packed in Istanbul, tracked to your door

Every order ships worldwide with tracking. What arrives is the piece we photographed: same hide, same wool, same grain.

The atelier

A workshop, not a warehouse.

Pure Grain is run by Emine from a small workshop in Istanbul, where every fur pillow, blanket, rug and bench cover is made to order or in small batches.

The same workshop supplies genuine fur and leather materials to fashion design studios, costume designers, film and television productions and craft projects: work that demands consistent quality, piece after piece.

When you write to the shop, you write to the person who made your piece. That is the whole supply chain.
